# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router): best fit for a multi-page marketing/portfolio site with shared layout, image optimization, and static export readiness for a luxury studio rebuild.

## Users

Primary: owners of villas and luxury apartments in Marbella (and Costa del Sol) who need interior design, author’s supervision, and FF&E procurement so the property looks better and sells faster.

Secondary: real-estate agents and property professionals referring high-end residential clients.

## Product Purpose

FISHEYE Architecture & Design is a Marbella-based interior architecture studio. The website’s job is to present the studio’s portfolio and process with enough credibility that a high-end residential prospect requests a project.

Success: a qualified visitor understands who FISHEYE is, trusts the work and process, and submits a project request or calls.

## Positioning

End-to-end interior projects for high-end homes in Marbella: design, on-site author’s supervision, and FF&E, with contractual schedules, dedicated project teams, and implementation accountability. Local presence in Nueva Andalucía, Marbella.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate work via project photography, read process/guarantees, check location and contact, then request a proposal (form, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, email). Projects are governed by electronic contracts with stage schedules updated daily.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site surfaces: Home, Projects (portfolio index + individual project pages), Work scheme, About, Contact, Request form, Privacy.
- Preserve factual content from the live site: project titles/descriptions, testimonials, stats (227 projects, 25 professionals, 14 years, 19 cities), process steps, reasons, contact details.
- Do not invent new commercial claims, prices, clients, or awards.
- Visual redesign replaces the incumbent Tilda look; product truth stays.

## Product Principles

1. Photography and built work do the selling; chrome stays quiet.
2. Every claim on the site must already exist in real studio content.
3. Process and accountability are part of the offer, not afterthoughts.
4. Contact paths stay obvious on every key surface.
5. Luxury is restraint: clarity, space, material honesty, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for marketing pages: semantic structure, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, meaningful alt text for project photography.
