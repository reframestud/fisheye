# Surface: Homepage Feedback

Mode: Persuade (home section)

Audience: Villa owners scanning for social proof before requesting
Job: Believe real clients left these words
Action: Continue toward request CTA
Proof: Four named testimonials with project places from site content
Constraints: Parchment Atelier only; no card grid; no invented ratings/avatars; do not touch pillars/workscheme/nav

## Direction contract

THESIS: Feedback reads as a guest-book of named clients — attribution leads, quote follows — refusing the article-column quote stack.

OWN-WORLD: Parchment/beige-2 bands, ink type, grey-200 hairlines; Instrument Serif name + quote; Inter uppercase place meta; oversized opening mark as review cue; no shadows or cards.

STORY: Visitor sees who spoke first, then what they said, and trusts the studio through named proof.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Section title Feedback; two-column ruled guest-book cells; each cell Name (serif ~1.35–1.85rem) over PLACE (0.75rem/0.08em uppercase Ash), then quote with large opening mark at body-quote scale.

FORM: Guest-book ledger, user-board THE ROLL / assumed after unanswered wait, seed key feedback-guestbook-ledger.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
